Support us

Sixty animals eat every day, and many of them are old. Feed, hay, pellets, supplements and the veterinary care that comes with an ageing herd are the bulk of what we spend.

We are not yet a registered charity, so donations are not currently tax-deductible. Your support makes a real difference right now: it provides the care our animals need today, while we work toward charitable status so we can grow what we do tomorrow.

Donate

Funds

Every dollar goes directly to the herd.

    One-time gifts of any amount.
    Monthly giving, which is what lets us plan feed by the season instead of by the week.
    Legacy gifts, for supporters who want to provide for the herd beyond their own lifetime. Please contact us directly.
    Raffles, drawn for specific needs through the year.
    Fundraising campaigns for one named thing at a time — a surgery, or a winter’s worth of hay.

We are proud to shop at Woolgrowers for pellets, supplements and medical supplies. Your donations are what make those everyday essentials possible.

Goods

In-kind donations are often seasonal, so keep an eye on our social media for what is needed right now. Past calls have included:

    Pools, for the alpacas in summer
    Pumpkins, in the autumn
    Radios, because they love music
    Old blankets, and gently used mini horse blankets

We work with a local hay and straw supplier, and we are always grateful when extra comes our way. Please talk to us before sending hay, because storage is the constraint.

Adopt a Camelid

Symbolic adoption supports one animal for a year. It does not bring the animal to your house, which people do ask. You choose your animal, and we send you their news: how they are doing, what they have been up to, and a little of their fibre after shearing.

Sponsor an Event or Program

Businesses, families and individuals can sponsor a single event or a full program for the season. Sponsorship covers materials, staffing and the cost of keeping places free for people who could not otherwise attend.
