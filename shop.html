About Camelid Beans

Camelid manure is called beans, and gardeners want it. It is low in odour, gentle enough to use without composting, and the single most common thing we are asked for.

We do not sell or give away beans, and we will not make an exception.

The reason is blastomycosis, a fungal infection caused by an organism that lives in damp soil and decaying organic matter. Disturbing contaminated material can release spores into the air, and inhaled spores can cause serious illness in people and animals alike. Moving manure off the farm moves that risk with it, into your garden and back into ours. Our manure is managed on site and stays here.

If you keep camelids yourself and want to know how we handle our piles, ask us. We would rather talk about it than pretend the question never comes up.
