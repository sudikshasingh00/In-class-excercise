Where We Are


The herd lives in the Lanark Highlands, Ontario, on the farm that has always been their home. We do not publish the address, because it is a private residence as well as a working farm. Everyone visiting gets directions when their visit is arranged.


Before You Write

A few things we are asked often, answered here to save you the email.

    We are not able to take in more animals at the moment. If you are looking for help with a camelid, write to us anyway and we will point you where we can.
    We do not breed, and no animal here is for sale.
    We do not sell beans. The Shop page explains why.
    Visits are by arrangement only. Please do not arrive unannounced — it is somebody’s home.
    Please leave dogs at home.
