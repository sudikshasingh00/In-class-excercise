Get Involved


We could not do this without help, and there is more than one way to give it.


Volunteer

Whether you can muck a barn, mend a fence, help on a trek, or lend a skill from home, there is a place for you here. We welcome volunteers of all abilities and backgrounds. No camelid experience is needed, and nobody is left alone with an animal before they are ready.

Where help is needed:

    Barn work — feeding, water, mucking out and turnout.
    Spa days — shearing, pedicures and the holding, brushing and reassuring that goes with them.
    Treks and visits — walking alongside guests and handling animals once you are trained.
    Grounds — fencing, mowing, and whatever the winter broke.
    From home — social media, writing, bookkeeping, photography and grant applications.

How to start:

    Get in touch and tell us roughly what you would like to do and when you are free.
    Come out for an orientation visit and meet the herd.
    Shadow a shift with an experienced volunteer.
    Settle into a regular shift, or stay on the list for busy weeks.

Become a Member

Membership will be one of the ways to support the herd year-round once we are incorporated, and one of the ways we stay accountable to the people who back us.

Lead a Workshop

If you have a skill you would like to share with a group — yoga, music, a craft class, painting, or something with the enormous amount of fibre we have to play with — get in touch. We are open to suggestions, and we will give you the space and the audience.

There is one requirement: it has to be llama approved.

Spread the Word

Follow us, share our story, and help more people find the herd. A share costs nothing and has brought us volunteers, hay and a pool.
